Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about Dholera investment

Is Dholera government-approved?

Yes. Dholera is a Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) project overseen by Gujarat Infrastructure Development Board (GIDB), headed by the Hon'ble Chief Minister of Gujarat. DICDL (Dholera Industrial City Development Ltd.) is the implementation agency with 51% equity from Government of Gujarat and 49% from Central Government (NICDIT).

What infrastructure is already complete?

The 22.54 Sq. Km Activation Area has 95% trunk infrastructure complete, including 72 km of internal roads, 50 MLD water treatment plant, 10 MLD sewage treatment plant, 220/33kV power substation, 135 km underground utility pipelines, and 200+ km underground power cabling.

Is this good for investment?

Yes. Dholera offers strong long-term appreciation potential due to its strategic DMIC location, ₹1 lakh crore committed investment, operational expressway (March 2026), upcoming international airport (Dec 2026), anchor industries like Tata Semiconductor, and government-backed infrastructure development.

What are the plot price ranges?

Plot prices vary by zone: Activation Area (Zone A) ₹18,000–₹28,000/sq.yd., Zone B (near airport) ₹12,000–₹18,000/sq.yd., Outer zones ₹8,000–₹12,000/sq.yd. Entry-level plots start from ₹6 lakh for 97 sq.yd. configurations.
